Giza City

Giza is a city in Egypt located on the bank of the Nile River. It is known for its magnificent location and extends along the river. Giza Governorate is a historic Egyptian region located on the west bank of the Nile, globally famous for the Giza Plateau. It hosts the iconic Great Pyramids and the Sphinx, attracting millions of visitors. Beyond its ancient wonders, Giza is a bustling urban hub, serving as a vital center for education, culture, and industry, while connecting Cairo to Upper Egypt.
