cairo City

Cairo is the capital city of Egypt located on the banks of the Nile River. It is known for its rich history, vibrant streets, and cultural significance. Cairo Governorate is one of the most important regions in the country, blending ancient heritage with modern urban life. It is home to famous landmarks such as the Egyptian Museum, Khan El Khalili Bazaar, and the historic Islamic and Coptic districts. Beyond its historical attractions, Cairo serves as Egypt’s political, educational, and economic center, welcoming millions of residents and visitors every year.
